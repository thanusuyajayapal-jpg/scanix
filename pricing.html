Patient Support

Billing FAQs

Common questions regarding costs, estimates, and insurance claims.

Will I receive an estimate before my scan?

Yes. Our billing department will provide a good-faith estimate outlining your expected out-of-pocket costs based on your specific insurance benefits before your scheduled appointment.

Are radiologist reading fees included?

Our self-pay pricing includes both the technical fee (the scan itself) and the professional fee (the radiologist's interpretation report). There are no separate hidden bills.

What if my scan requires IV contrast?

If your referring physician orders a scan "with contrast," there is an additional charge for the contrast material and administration. We will inform you of this cost beforehand.

Do you require a doctor's referral?

Yes, a valid prescription or order from a licensed healthcare provider is required for all diagnostic imaging, regardless of whether you are using insurance or self-paying.

Section 01

General & Appointments

You can easily book your appointment through our online patient portal, or by calling our scheduling department directly at 1-800-555-SCAN. We also accept walk-ins for routine X-rays, subject to availability.

Yes. For your safety and to comply with medical regulations, all advanced imaging procedures (MRI, CT, Ultrasound) require a valid referral or prescription from a licensed healthcare provider prior to your scan.

Please bring your photo ID, current insurance card, the physical prescription/referral from your doctor, and any previous relevant imaging records (on CD or film) if they were performed at an outside facility.

Yes, you can reschedule or cancel your appointment securely through our patient portal up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled slot without incurring any administrative fees. For changes within 24 hours, please contact our support desk directly.

We work with all major healthcare insurance networks. We recommend checking your policy details or contacting our billing department ahead of time to verify pre-authorization requirements for your specific diagnostic test.

Modalities

Advanced Imaging Technologies

Beyond basic diagnostics, SCANIX provides comprehensive imaging utilizing the industry's most advanced equipment, minimizing scan times while maximizing image clarity.

High-Field 3T MRI

Our 3 Tesla Magnetic Resonance Imaging scanner provides extraordinary detail for neurological, musculoskeletal, and body imaging. The wider bore design significantly reduces claustrophobia while completing scans up to 40% faster than standard machines.

  • Exceptional Detail: Twice the signal strength of traditional MRI.
  • Patient Comfort: Wide-bore design for reduced anxiety.

Ultra-Fast Low-Dose CT

Our multi-slice Computed Tomography scanner generates highly detailed 3D views of your anatomy in seconds. Utilizing advanced dose-reduction software, we minimize radiation exposure without compromising the critical diagnostic quality required by specialists.

  • Safety First: Up to 60% lower radiation dose than standard CT.
  • Rapid Scans: Entire body scans completed in under a minute.
